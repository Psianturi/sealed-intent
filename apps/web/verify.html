Verify a receipt

Drop in a receipt and this page checks it right here in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere. A valid receipt means a Sealed Intent instance really made that decision and nobody changed it afterwards. The amount and the receiver stay sealed unless the owner opens one field for you.

What is checked

  • Issuer signature: ed25519 over the canonical JSON of the receipt. Any edit, even one character, invalidates it.
  • Issuer identity: only if you paste the key the operator published. Without it the receipt is self-signed by an unknown key and this page says so.
  • Enclave signature: the signed text must equal model:sha256(request):sha256(response) and recover to the signing address. Offline-simulated receipts are labelled as such and never count as attested. Real receipts must carry a locally verified attestation; the Intel and NVIDIA checks themselves happen at decision time and are listed in the receipt.
  • Disclosure: a disclosed field must match its salted commitment.
