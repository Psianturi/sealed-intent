          What the check-up looks at

          Who can sign

          Every full-access key can move everything. More than one you do not recognise is the first sign of a takeover.

          App connections

          Each site you log in to leaves a key on your account. Keys to look-alike, reported or deleted apps are cut.

          Bait

          Look-alike tokens and unsolicited NFTs carry text meant to make a person, or an agent, pay. SeIn names them.

          Contacts

          Recent counterparties are checked against SeIn's list of reported accounts and the names of well-known contracts.

          History

          The last 50 transactions are read for the patterns that come before a loss: look-alike senders, planted dust, lure contracts and one-off receivers.

          Read-only until you choose to heal. Data from NEAR RPC, FastNEAR and NearBlocks, read by your browser. SeIn's list of reported accounts starts small and grows from what its agents report; a clean result is not a guarantee.
