A contract on NEAR

SeIn's registry is a smart contract on NEAR testnet. It lists the keys that sign receipts and the agents, and it holds a fingerprint of every batch of receipts, so a receipt cannot be changed or made up later.

    Payments through OutLayer custody

    The agent never holds a key. Money moves only through an OutLayer custody wallet, which signs the transaction. Each payment below is read from the chain, with who signed it and who was paid.

      An opinion from a NEAR AI Cloud enclave

      For every payment SeIn asks a model running inside a hardware enclave on NEAR AI Cloud whether the request looks like a trick. The enclave signs its answer. Your browser checks that signature, then asks NEAR AI which key its enclave uses today.

        Prices from NEAR Intents

        Before a swap SeIn asks NEAR Intents (1Click) for its own quote and checks the solver network's signature, so a made-up price cannot pass. Ask for a fresh quote and watch your browser check it.

          Owner approval with a NEP-413 wallet signature

          When SeIn is unsure, only the owner can let a payment through, by signing a message with their NEAR wallet. The message names the decision and a fingerprint of exactly what the owner was shown, so a signature cannot be moved to another payment.
